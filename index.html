HBT102 — Basic Skills in Interpreting

Tutorial Week 2

Dr. Nor Idatul Akmar Zulkipli

Learning Focus

This foundational exercise develops divided attention and working memory as cognitive skills for managing multiple tasks during interpreting.

1. Listening

Listen carefully to the audio clip. Focus entirely on the speaker's main points, tone, and logical flow. Do not take notes or speak.

Listen once with full attention. When you have finished, mark this stage complete.

2. Shadowing

Listen to the audio again. This time, shadow the speaker aloud with a Décalage
The slight delay between hearing the speaker and repeating the words during shadowing. while the speaker is still speaking. Maintain a steady pace and match the speaker's rhythm.

Playback speed:

Start at 0.85× if you need support. Once you can maintain a steady 2–3-word lag, repeat the task at 1.0×.

Important: You must continue speaking while the speaker is speaking. Do not wait until the speaker finishes a sentence before repeating it.
Complete Listening first to unlock this stage.

3. Writing — Divided Attention

Continue shadowing the audio. While speaking, start writing numbers in ascending order on your paper.

Take Note: Do not stop shadowing in order to write, and do not stop writing in order to shadow. Keep both tasks moving simultaneously.

This is the most demanding stage. You must simultaneously:

  • Listen to the audio.
  • Shadow with a 2–3-word lag.
  • Write ascending numbers by hand on paper.

Note: Write on physical paper. No digital text-entry box is provided.

Start writing as soon as you begin shadowing. Keep both tasks going until the audio ends.

Complete Shadowing first to unlock this stage.

4. Recall

Do not replay the audio before answering.

Without listening to the text again, recall as much information as you can.

Question 1

What was the main issue discussed?

Question 2

What were the API readings in those locations?

Question 3

What advice was given to the public?

Complete Writing first to unlock Recall.
